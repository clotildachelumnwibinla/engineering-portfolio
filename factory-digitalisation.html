Factory Digitalisation & Scanning

Supporting the digital representation of factory environments and monitoring adoption of digital factory tools.

Factory ViewerPower BIAWS AthenaCDH

Project context

Factory scanning and digitalisation support production planning by making factory information more accessible and easier to work with.

My contribution

  • Managed and supported the Factory Viewer platform.
  • Supported factory scanning and digitalisation activities.
  • Created and managed a Power BI dashboard for user analytics.
  • Analysed monthly platform usage across departments, sections and Ressorts.
  • Worked with AWS Athena and CDH in the context of data access and analysis.

Outcome

The dashboard provided a structured view of monthly user activity and platform adoption, helping stakeholders understand usage across organisational areas.
