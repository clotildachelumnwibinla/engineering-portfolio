MASTER'S THESIS · FAU / BMW GROUP

Image-Based AI for Battery Manufacturing

Comparative Analysis of Image-Based AI Methods for Fault Classification in Thermal Adhesive Application for High-Voltage Battery Cell Clusters.

PythonJupyterYOLOPyTorchComputer vision

Research context

The thesis investigates image-based approaches for identifying and classifying faults in thermal adhesive application within high-voltage battery cell-cluster manufacturing.

Research objectives

  • Develop and optimise different image-based AI approaches for automated fault detection and classification.
  • Compare approaches using defined performance and operational criteria.
  • Assess suitability for reliable use in an industrial manufacturing environment.

Methods & tools

Python · Jupyter Notebook · YOLO-based computer vision · Image classification · Model training and evaluation.

Evaluation

F1-score · Recall · Defect detection rate · Processing time · Robustness · Scalability · Interpretability · Potential for process optimisation.
