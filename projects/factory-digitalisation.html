BMW · INDUSTRIAL DIGITALISATION

Factory digitalisation & scanning

Factory Viewer platform management, digital factory scanning activities and Power BI user analytics in an industrial production environment.

Project overview

The work focused on managing and improving the use of digital tools that support factory visualisation and digitalisation. A central responsibility was the Factory Viewer platform and the analysis of its usage across organisational areas.

Activities & contributions

  • Managed the Factory Viewer platform in the context of factory digitalisation and scanning.
  • Created and managed a Power BI dashboard to analyse platform usage.
  • Prepared monthly user analysis across departments, sections and organisational areas (Ressorts).
  • Worked with Power BI, AWS Athena and CDH in data-related workflows.
  • Used collaboration tools such as Confluence and Jira.
Publication note
BMW internal information, factory layouts, screenshots, production data and process-specific details are not included in this public portfolio.

Skills & tools
