Factory Digitalisation & Scanning

Factory Viewer platform management, scanning and digitalisation support, and Power BI user analytics in a production environment.

Project overview

This work focused on factory digitalisation and the use of digital platforms and analytics to support production environments. A key part of the role was managing the Factory Viewer platform and creating visibility into how the platform was used across the organisation.

Activities & contributions

  • Managed the Factory Viewer platform as part of factory digitalisation and scanning activities.
  • Created and managed a Power BI dashboard to analyse platform usage.
  • Prepared monthly user analysis across different departments, sections and organisational areas (Ressorts).
  • Worked with Power BI, AWS Athena and CDH in the context of production digitalisation and data workflows.
  • Collaborated through tools including Confluence and Jira.
