Master's Thesis

AI-Based Fault Classification

Image-based AI for fault classification in thermal adhesive application for high-voltage battery cell clusters.

Project overview

The master's thesis investigated an image-based AI approach for identifying and classifying faults in thermal adhesive application for high-voltage battery cell clusters.

Activities & contributions

  • Investigated image-based methods for manufacturing-related fault classification.
  • Worked with Python and Jupyter for data and model development.
  • Used YOLO and PyTorch for computer-vision and deep-learning workflows.
  • Used MATLAB for supporting analysis and engineering work where applicable.
  • Focused the research on a manufacturing quality problem in a high-voltage battery context.
