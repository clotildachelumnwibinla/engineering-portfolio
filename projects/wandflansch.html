BACHELOR'S THESIS · FH AACHEN

Automated Manufacturing Information for a Wall Flange

Developing a procedure to automate the provision of design-related manufacturing information and support more efficient machining preparation.

Mechanical engineeringDesign for ManufacturingEXAPTsolid

Project context

The thesis explored how design-related information can support manufacturing decisions, using a wall-flange component as a case study.

My contribution

  • Created a procedure for automated provision of design-related manufacturing information.
  • Conducted Design for Manufacturing analysis.
  • Developed and assessed 26 design variants.
  • Derived feedback information to support automated design improvements.

Tools & methods

SolidWorks · EXAPTsolid · CAD modelling · DFM analysis · Manufacturing information automation.
